==============================================================================================================
KEY LIMITATIONS AND HONEST INTERPRETATIONS
==============================================================================================================

1. GAM / Spearman correlations (Step 3):
   All-station analyses show flat/non-significant relationships (r≈0.00-0.04).
   This is NOT an artefact — it reflects genuine spatial and historical noise
   overwhelming the DO gradient signal at the full SIS scale.
   The DO signal emerges only in the stressed subset (DO≤4): Pielou r=+0.370***.
   Interpretation: DO structures evenness within stressed zones, not across all
   stations where spatial turnover dominates community assembly.

2. K-means clustering (Step 2):
   Hiroshima (silhouette=0.409): acceptable cluster separation; results robust.
   Osaka (silhouette=0.368): borderline — 6 clusters likely overfits spatial noise.
   SIS sediment (silhouette=0.276): WEAK separation — sediment types are
   continuously distributed, not discretely clustered. Cluster labels are
   descriptive summaries only, not ecologically distinct regimes.

3. Mantel tests (Step 4):
   Mantel r values (~0.07-0.18) are statistically significant but explain <4%
   of community dissimilarity variance. Mean Bray-Curtis ≈ 0.947 indicates
   very high community turnover across the SIS — environmental gradients
   explain a minority of this variation. Consistent with dispersal limitation
   and stochastic assembly operating alongside environmental filtering.

4. Random Forest (Step 5) — 3-class stress classification:
   Continuous Shannon regression was discarded (R²=0.066, zero-inflation failure).
   Replaced with 3-class classifier: dead (DO≤2) / stressed (2-4) / healthy (DO>4).
   Results: overall accuracy=70.4%, F1-weighted=0.708 (vs 33% random baseline).
   Healthy stations: F1=0.840 — model reliably identifies normoxic zones.
   Dead zones: F1=0.243 — sediment chemistry lags DO recovery; recovering stations
   retain anaerobic sediment signatures and are misclassified as 'dead'.
   This lag itself is an ecological finding: benthic recovery is slower than
   water column re-oxygenation. Top MDI feature: Depth_m (stratification proxy)
   followed by ORP, LOI, Sulfides — all encoding anaerobic sediment history.
   RF used for MDI driver ranking only. Confusion matrix is the primary output.

5. Overarching interpretation:
   'Environmental variables (DO, TOC, Sulfides) structure COMMUNITY COMPOSITION
   at the assemblage level [Mantel r significant], but are insufficient to
   predict LOCAL DIVERSITY at individual stations [R²=0.066].'
   This mirrors findings in spatially complex semi-enclosed systems where
   beta-diversity is driven by multiple interacting processes beyond simple
   environmental filtering — a key distinction from the Pearl River Estuary
   where DO gradients are steeper and spatially more coherent.
